# Subscription Plan Upgrades and Downgrades

## 1. Available Broadband Tiers
- **Starter Fibre**: 100 Mbps down / 50 Mbps up ($45/month)
- **UltraFibre**: 500 Mbps down / 250 Mbps up ($65/month)
- **Gigabit Pro**: 1 Gbps down / 500 Mbps up ($85/month)
- **HyperFibre 2.5G**: 2.5 Gbps symmetrical ($110/month, requires Wi-Fi 6E/7 gateway upgrade)

## 2. Upgrade Rules
- **Speed Activation**: Upgrades take effect within 15 minutes of confirmation; no technician visit required if existing ONT supports Gigabit.
- **Billing Mechanics**: First month charges are prorated from the day of activation.
- **Contract Term**: Standard upgrades do not reset existing 12-month or 24-month contract terms.

## 3. Downgrades
- Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle. No mid-cycle downgrades are processed.
