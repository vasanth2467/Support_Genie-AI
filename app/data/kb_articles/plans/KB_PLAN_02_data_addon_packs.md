# On-Demand Mobile Data Boost Packs

## 1. Overview
Customers who exhaust their monthly high-speed mobile data allotment can purchase immediate one-time data boosters rather than experiencing speed throttling to 128 kbps.

## 2. Pack Options and Pricing
- **Speed Boost 2GB**: $5.00 (Valid for 7 calendar days or until end of billing cycle)
- **Power Boost 10GB**: $15.00 (Valid for 30 calendar days)
- **Unlimited Weekend Pass**: $8.00 (Unlimited high-speed from Friday 6:00 PM to Sunday 11:59 PM)

## 3. Instant Activation Protocol
1. Verify customer mobile line status and remaining high-speed data balance.
2. Present available boost pack pricing clearly.
3. Upon explicit customer confirmation ("Yes, add 10GB"), add the charge to the ledger and provision the quota immediately.
