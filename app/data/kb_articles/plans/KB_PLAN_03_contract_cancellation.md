# Service Cancellation and Early Termination Guidelines

## 1. Statutory Cooling-Off Window
New residential subscribers have a statutory **14-calendar-day cooling-off window** from service activation to cancel without incurring Early Termination Fees (ETFs). Provided equipment must be returned in original condition within 14 days.

## 2. Standard Contract Term Commitments
- **Month-to-Month**: Requires 30 days written notice; zero cancellation penalties.
- **12/24-Month Fixed Term Agreements**:
  - Early Termination Fee equals $15 per remaining contract month up to a maximum cap of $180.
  - Fees are automatically waived in cases of verified relocation to an area not covered by our network or customer bereavement.

## 3. Mandatory Human Retention Escalation
AI assistants are **NOT permitted** to finalize account cancellations or terminate contracts directly.
- Whenever a customer explicitly expresses the intent to cancel or terminate service, the assistant must gather the primary reason (price, service quality, moving home), summarize customer tenure/value, and escalate to the **Customer Retention & Loyalty Team**.
