# International Roaming Activation and Pass Management

## 1. Roaming Readiness
To use voice, text, and mobile data outside your domestic home network, International Roaming must be switched ON at the network level and on your handset.

## 2. Global Day Pass Pricing
- **Zone 1 (North America / Europe / Asia Pacific)**: $10/day for unlimited talk, text, and 2GB high-speed daily data (reduced to 256kbps thereafter).
- Charges trigger only on calendar days when roaming data, outbound call, or SMS is used abroad.

## 3. Troubleshooting "No Service Abroad"
1. Confirm network roaming switch is enabled in the customer profile.
2. In device settings: Turn ON **Data Roaming** under Cellular Settings.
3. If auto-network selection fails, manually select a partner network (e.g. Vodafone, Orange, NTT Docomo, Telstra).
4. Restart handset to complete location registration with the foreign VLR (Visitor Location Register).
