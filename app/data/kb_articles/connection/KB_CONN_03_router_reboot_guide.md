# Wi-Fi Gateway and Router Power Cycle Guide

## 1. When to Restart Your Gateway
A controlled power cycle clears corrupted NAT tables, frees exhausted RAM, and requests a fresh DHCP lease and DNS mapping from the broadband gateway.

## 2. Recommended Procedure (The 30-30 Rule)
1. Turn off the power switch on the back of the router, or unplug the AC power cord from the wall outlet.
2. Wait a full **30 seconds**. Do not rush this step, as lingering current can keep volatile RAM chips partially energized.
3. Plug the power cord back into the wall outlet.
4. Allow **3 to 5 minutes** for the indicator lights (Power, WAN/Internet, 2.4G, 5G) to stabilize to solid white or green.
5. Reconnect your client device and verify connectivity.

## 3. Persistent Loop Limitation
- If the customer reports restarting the router more than twice in the ongoing session without restoration of service, the assistant must avoid suggesting another reboot and escalate to Tier-2 Technical Support.
