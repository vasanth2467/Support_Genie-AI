# Slow Broadband Speeds and Wi-Fi Optimization

## 1. Problem Overview
Slow speed complaints can originate from Wi-Fi interference, high network congestion, bandwidth hogging, or hardware throttling.

## 2. Diagnostic Steps
1. **Differentiate Wi-Fi vs. Wired**:
   - Instruct the customer to test with an Ethernet cable directly connected to the router if possible.
   - Run a standardized speed test at `speedtest.net` with background downloads paused.
2. **Wi-Fi Band Separation & Channel Selection**:
   - Ensure devices demanding high bandwidth (gaming consoles, 4K TVs, work laptops) connect to the **5 GHz** or **6 GHz** Wi-Fi band rather than the congested 2.4 GHz band.
   - Enable Auto-Channel selection in the router settings to evade neighbor frequency overlap.
3. **Router Placement**:
   - Verify router is elevated at least 3 feet off the floor, in an open area away from thick concrete walls, mirrors, microwaves, and metal filing cabinets.
4. **Resolution Summary**:
   - If wired speeds meet 80% or more of the subscribed plan rate (e.g. 400 Mbps on a 500 Mbps plan), the line is healthy and the issue is Wi-Fi range. Recommend a Wi-Fi 6 Mesh Extender.
   - If wired speeds are below 20% of subscribed rate persistently, escalate to Network Engineering for line SNR audit.
