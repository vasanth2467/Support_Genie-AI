# Customer Refund and Courtesy Credit Policy

## 1. Eligibility Criteria
Refunds and credits are governed by corporate finance compliance rules to ensure fair customer treatment while preventing unauthorized write-offs.

## 2. Automated Resolution Rules (Tier-1 Assistant)
- **Threshold Limit**: SupportGenie AI is authorized to approve automated courtesy credits of up to **$50.00 USD** per incident.
- **Frequency Restriction**: Maximum 1 courtesy credit per customer account within any 180-day window.
- **Valid Scenarios**: Service interruption lasting over 6 hours (prorated credit), accidental one-off pay-per-view purchase reported within 48 hours, or incorrect equipment delivery surcharge.

## 3. Mandatory Human Escalation Thresholds
Under the following conditions, the AI assistant **MUST NOT** approve a refund and must generate an escalation ticket to the Billing Operations Team:
- Requested refund or disputed amount exceeds **$50.00 USD**.
- Customer has received a prior courtesy refund in the past 6 months.
- Refund requested as direct bank wire or credit card reversal (rather than ledger account credit).
- Cancellation and final balance refund on terminated accounts.

## 4. Processing Timelines
- Ledger Credits: Applied immediately to next monthly cycle.
- Bank Card Reversals (Human Approved): 3 to 5 business days depending on the financial institution.
