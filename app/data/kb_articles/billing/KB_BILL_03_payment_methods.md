# Payment Methods and Auto-Pay Management

## 1. Accepted Payment Channels
- **Credit/Debit Cards**: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover.
- **Direct Debit / ACH**: Bank routing and checking account number.
- **Digital Wallets**: Apple Pay, Google Pay via the customer web portal.

## 2. Auto-Pay Discount & Billing Schedule
- Enrolling in Auto-Pay provides an ongoing **$5.00/month discount** on eligible broadband and mobile plans.
- Deductions occur automatically 3 business days prior to the billing cycle due date.

## 3. Resolving Declined or Failed Payments
- **Retry Window**: Failed payments are automatically retried 48 hours later.
- **Grace Period**: Accounts retain active connectivity for 7 calendar days after a missed payment before soft suspension.
- **Reconnection**: Updating card details and clearing the balance immediately restores service within 15 minutes.
