# Disputed Charges and Billing Investigation Process

## 1. Overview
Customers may question unexpected charges on their monthly invoice, including roaming surcharges, data overage fees, service add-on fees, or proration adjustments after plan changes.

## 2. Common Causes of Billing Variance
- **Prorated Charges**: When an account plan is altered mid-cycle, charges are calculated proportionally for remaining billing cycle days.
- **Data Overage**: If an account on a capped data plan exceeds the monthly threshold, automated add-on packs ($10 per 5GB) are applied.
- **Unrecognized Surcharges / Service Add-ons**: Add-on features, international roaming fees, or third-party telecom subscriptions billed to the account.
- **Late Payment Assessment**: A $15 late fee applies if balance remains unpaid 10 days past due date.

## 3. Grounded Resolution Protocol
1. **Verify Account Details**: Confirm the customer's identity and load the latest billing breakdown.
2. **Identify the Line Item**: Request specific details: the invoice date and the line-item name/amount.
3. **Check Courtesy Eligibility**:
   - If the disputed charge is for an accidental service add-on or one-time data overage under **$50.00**, and the customer has had zero disputes in the preceding 6 months, an automated courtesy credit may be issued.
   - For disputed charges **exceeding $50.00** or recurring monthly plan discrepancies, the dispute must be escalated to a Billing Supervisor for human audit.
4. **Citation Reference**: Section 3.2 of the Fair Billing Guidelines (POL-FIN-201).
